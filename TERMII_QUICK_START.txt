NEXT STEP - Register Your Sender ID:
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://termii.com/dashboard

2. Log in with your account credentials

3. Navigate to: Configuration → Sender ID (or Settings → Sender ID)

4. Click "Request New Sender ID"

5. Fill in the form:
   - Sender ID Name: KhanInvoice
   - Purpose: Transactional (invoice notifications)
   - Company/Business: Khan Invoice

6. Submit required documents (usually):
   - Business registration certificate (CAC)
   - Valid ID of business owner
   - Letter of authorization (Termii may provide template)

7. Wait for approval: Usually 24-48 hours (sometimes same day)

8. Once approved, test again with:
   ssh root@147.182.242.177
   cd /var/www/kinvoice.ng
   php artisan test:messaging +2348168166109

ALTERNATIVE - Use Numeric Sender ID (Faster Approval):
================================================================================

If you want FASTER approval (often instant):

1. Request a NUMERIC sender ID instead (e.g., "2348012345678")
2. These are approved much faster but look less professional
3. Messages will show from a phone number instead of "KhanInvoice"

FOR WHATSAPP:
================================================================================

WhatsApp requires additional setup in Termii:
1. Go to Channels → WhatsApp in Termii dashboard
2. Connect your WhatsApp Business account
3. Complete WhatsApp Business verification
4. This can take 3-5 business days

TESTING AFTER APPROVAL:
================================================================================

Once your Sender ID is approved:

1. No code changes needed - everything is ready!

2. Test SMS:
   php artisan test:messaging +2348168166109 --sms

3. Expected output:
   ✓ SMS sent successfully!
   Message ID: 123456789
   Remaining balance: ₦8

4. Check if recipient received the SMS

5. If successful, SMS notifications are LIVE for:
   - Invoice sent notifications
   - Payment received notifications
   - Payment reminders
   - Overdue invoice alerts
